About Our School

Miniland Academy English School located in Kerabari-7, Dangi, Morang, is committed to nurturing young minds from Play Group to Class 9. Our mission is to provide holistic education blending modern pedagogy with moral values.

We believe in creating future leaders through a supportive environment, experienced faculty, and a vibrant campus life. Our motto "Morality, Discipline & Quality Education" drives everything we do.

Our Vision

To be a center of educational excellence that empowers students with confidence, knowledge, and integrity to excel globally.

Academic Programs

  • Early Years: Play Group & Nursery – Fun-based learning
  • Primary Wing: Classes 1-5 – Strong foundation in core subjects
  • Lower Secondary: Classes 6-8 – Subject specialization
  • Secondary Prep: Class 9 – SEE oriented curriculum
  • Co-curricular: Sports, Music, Art, Debate & Computer Lab
