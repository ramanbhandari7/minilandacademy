About Our School

Miniland Academy English School, located in Kerabari 7, Dangi, Morang, is committed to quality education. Our vision is a learning environment that nurtures young minds for future challenges.

We believe in holistic education: academic excellence and character building. Our experienced faculty help each student reach their full potential.

Our Mission

To provide a stimulating learning environment promoting academic excellence, creativity, and moral values, preparing students to be responsible citizens and future leaders.

Our Programs

We offer comprehensive educational programs:

  • Early Childhood: Play Group & Nursery (Play-based learning)
  • Primary Level: Classes 1-5 (Balanced curriculum)
  • Lower Secondary: Classes 6-8 (Specialized teachers)
  • Secondary: Class 9 (Preparing for SEE)
  • Co-curricular: Sports, arts, music, clubs
