About Miniland Academy

Miniland Academy is a premier educational institution in Kathmandu, Nepal, established in 2003 with a vision to provide quality education that nurtures young minds and prepares them for the challenges of tomorrow.

We believe in a holistic approach to education that combines academic excellence with character development, creativity, and critical thinking. Our curriculum is designed to meet international standards while being rooted in Nepali values and culture.

Our Academic Programs

Comprehensive education from Playgroup to Grade 9

Playgroup

Age 2+ years

Sensory play, motor skills development, social interaction, and introduction to learning through fun activities.

Nursery

Age 3+ years

Language development, basic numeracy, creative expression, and building social skills through group activities.

LKG & UKG

Age 4-5 years

Foundation in literacy and numeracy, phonetics, writing skills, and preparation for formal schooling.
